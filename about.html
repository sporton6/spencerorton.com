About Spencer Orton

Spencer Orton (he/him) is an actor, singer and dancer based in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, and raised in the small town of Pittsford, New York. He is on track to finish his BFA in Musical Theater at Point Park University in May 2027.

Spencer found his love for performing early, when the dazzling movie Frozen came out. He was often caught annoying his grandmother by singing “Let It Go” at all hours of the day. That passion grew quickly late in high school with Spring Awakening, a show he fell in love with.

Off the stage, you can find Spencer rooting for the New York Knicks and the Buffalo Bills, going on walks, hitting the gym, and probably wearing a ringer T-shirt.
